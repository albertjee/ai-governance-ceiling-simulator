How to Use This Simulator

This is a quick walkthrough of every control and result on the page. No account, install, or internet connection is required — everything runs locally in your browser.

1. Pick a starting estimate

The five cards at the top describe five stages of AI governance maturity, from Stage 1 — Ad Hoc (informal, no clear ownership) to Stage 5 — Adaptive / Optimizing (continuously improved and deeply evidenced). Read through them and use the Starting maturity estimate slider to set the number that best matches where your organization sits today. Most organizations land somewhere between two stages, and that's fine — the slider moves in small steps for exactly that reason.

2. Adjust the five maturity domains

Below the starting estimate are five sliders, one per area. Move each one to reflect your honest assessment:

  • Governance — policy, ownership, and oversight of AI use.
  • Technical Capability — how mature your actual AI tooling and response practices are.
  • Evidence Confidence — how much of your governance claims you could actually prove with documentation.
  • NHI Lifecycle — how well you manage non-human identities (service accounts, API keys, and AI agents) from creation to retirement.
  • Logging / Traceability — whether AI activity is actually logged and reviewable after the fact.

3. Try improvements

Each toggle represents one concrete control you could put in place — things like naming an AI risk owner or adding SIEM logging evidence. Flip a few on and watch how the results shift. This is meant to help you see which single change would move your score the most.

4. Read the results

The right-hand panel updates instantly as you move things:

  • Raw capability score — your inputs combined, before any discounting.
  • Evidence-adjusted score — the raw score after a penalty for weak evidence or logging.
  • Final capped score — the number that actually counts. It can be held down — "capped" — by governance, NHI lifecycle, or logging maturity even if your raw score is higher.
  • Governance Ceiling status — tells you plainly whether something is currently holding your score back.
  • Primary blocker and Next unlock — your weakest domain right now, and the single most direct way to raise it.
  • What moves the score most? — the top three improvements, ranked by estimated impact, based on your current settings.
  • Domain Maturity Profile — a bar for each of the five domains, with a vertical marker showing where your final capped score sits relative to all of them.

5. Start over anytime

The Reset to baseline button restores every slider and toggle to its default value, so you can always get back to a clean starting point.

This is a heuristic what-if model for exploration and discussion.

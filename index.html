AI Security Governance Ceiling Simulator

Estimate how governance maturity can cap AI security capability, then test which policy, ownership, evidence, and response improvements unlock the next maturity stage.

This is a heuristic what-if simulator, not an evidence-based tenant assessment.

Use these stage descriptions to choose your starting estimate. Most organizations fall between stages.

Stage 1 - Ad Hoc AI use is informal, ownership is unclear, and evidence is mostly anecdotal or unavailable.
Stage 2 - Defined Basic policy exists, known owners are emerging, and high-risk use cases are tracked manually.
Stage 3 - Managed Cross-functional review, inventories, risk acceptance, and repeatable control evidence are in place.
Stage 4 - Measured Telemetry, lifecycle controls, and response playbooks are tested against measurable AI risk indicators.
Stage 5 - Adaptive / Optimizing Controls are continuously improved, deeply evidenced, and aligned to business-critical AI operations.
